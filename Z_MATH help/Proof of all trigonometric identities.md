Proof that $\sin(90+\theta) = \cos(\theta)$ and $\cos(90+\theta) = -\sin(\theta)$

The point of the +90 triangle is the same distance from the y axis as the point of the regular triangle is from the x axis. Because the triangle is inverted, this is $-\sin(\theta)$.
The point on the +90 is the same distance from the x axis as the regular triangle is from the y axis. There is no inversion, so it is $\cos(\theta)$

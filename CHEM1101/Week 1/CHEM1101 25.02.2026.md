Energies of [[Orbital]]s
For an one electron hydrogen atom, orbitals on the same energy level(value of n) have the same energy. 

![[Degenerate orbitals]]

In multi electron systems
As the number of electrons increase, so does the repulsion between them 
Therefore, in atoms with more than one electron, not all orbitals on the same energy are degenerate. As electron numbers increase, energy levels start to overlap. (4s is lower in energy than 3d). 

Filling orbitals
In a **multi electron atom** (not hydrogen), no two electrons in the same atom can have an identical 4 set of [[Quantum numbers]]. This means that every electron in an atom must differ by at least one of the four quantum number values.

Quantum mechanics
Describes the behaviour of sub atomic particles
Electrons can teleport all the time 
Observing the position of electrons will change their position.
Electrons can act as both waves and light
Quantum mechanical model allows to understand why certain compounds act in certain ways

Erwin Schrödinger developed quantum mechanics as a mathematical treatment into which both a wave and particle could be incorporated
Electrons are quantum objects. An electron is partly a wave and partly a particle.
Electrons are most dense near the nucleus.

 
The solution to the schrödinger wave equation is $\psi$
$\psi ^2$ gives the election density, or the probability of finding an electron at a location at an instant in time.

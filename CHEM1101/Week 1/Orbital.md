Orbital
An orbital is described by its set of [[Quantum numbers]]
Remember that each orbital can contain 2 electrons
![[Quantum numbers]]An orbital is often described as a blob of uncertainty. 
Orbitals take a shape where there is a high probability **(90%)** of finding an electron. 
electrons do not orbit the nucleus, an orbital is simply a designated area that an electron has a high probability of appearing in.

electron configuration
The way electrons are distributed in at atom is its electron config.
The most stable organisation is the lowest possible energy, called the **ground state**. 

Each component consists of a number denoting the energy level, a letter denoting the type of orbital, a superscript denoting the number of electrons in those [[Orbital]]s. EG $4p^5$ denotes 5 electrons in the 4p orbital.

Electron filling order should follow [[Hunds rule]] and the [[n+L rule]].

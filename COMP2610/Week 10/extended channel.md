# Definition 
The $N^{th}$ extended [[channel]] of $Q$ from $\mathcal{X}\to \mathcal{Y}$ is a channel from $\mathcal{X}^N$ to $\mathcal{Y}^N$, with transition probability $\mathbf{x}\in \mathcal{X}^N$ to $\mathbf{y}\in \mathcal{Y}^N$ given by $$P(\mathbf{y|\mathbf{x}})= \prod_{n=1}^N P(\mathbf{y}_{i}|\mathbf{x}_{i})$$
The idea is that as $N$ increases, **any** [[extended channel]] looks like the [[Discrete channel#The noisy typewriter channel|the noisy typewriter channel]].

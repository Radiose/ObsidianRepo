conditional expectation
the conditional expectation is very similar to the regular [[Random variable|expected value]], except it takes into account [[Conditional probability]].

To be precise, $\mathbb{E}[X | Y=y]$ gives you an expected value for each $y$.
The discrete definition is $\mathbb{E}[X | Y=y]=\sum_{x} xP(X=x|Y=y)$

An [[ensemble]] $X$ is a triple $(x,\mathcal{A}_{x},\mathcal{P}_{x})$, where $x$ is a [[random variable]] taking values in $\mathcal{A}_{X}=\{ a_{1},\dots,a_{l} \}$(its alphabet) with probabilities $\mathcal{P}_{X}=\{ p_{1},\dots,p_{n} \}$

The [[information]] in the observation that $x=a_{i}$ in the ensemble $x$ is given by $h(a_{i})=\log_{2} \frac{1}{p_{i}}=-\log_{2} p_{i}$

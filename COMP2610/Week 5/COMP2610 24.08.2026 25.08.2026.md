Today: More information theory inequalities. 


Recall that expectation is linear. However, variance is not typically linear. 
$var\left( \sum_{i=1}^n X_{i}\right)=\sum_{i=1}^nvar(X_{i})$ does not hold if $X_{i}$ as a family of random variables are not [[Independent event|independent]].

Additionally, $var(aX)=a^2var(X)$

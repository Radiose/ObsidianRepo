Sums and products of terms 
$\sum_{n=1}^k a_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}\dots + a_{k}$

$\prod_{n=1}^k a_{n}=a_{1}\times a_{2}...a_{k}$ = product 

Given a sequence, we can define a **series** as the sum of the sequence.
$\sum_{n\in i}a_{n}$
We can also consider a partial sum of the series which is just a series associated to some *subsequence*, or in other words, typically the sum of some finite length of the [[sequence]].

Geometric [[Sequence]]
[[implicit definition of a sequence|implicit definition]] 
$a_{k}=a$ (first term)
$a_{n+1}=ra_{n},\forall_{n}\ge k$
You can get the [[Explicit definition of a Sequence|explicit definition]] from the [[implicit definition of a sequence|implicit definition]] via mathematical [[Induction]]
[[Explicit definition of a Sequence|explicit definition]]
$\forall_{n} \ge k$
$a_{n}=ar^{n-k}$
So a geometric sequence is basically just a sequence with a common ratio in from of each term that typically increases with each term.

Geometric series of N terms 
$\sum_{n=k}^{k+(N-1)}ar^{n-k}=a+ar+\dots ar^{N-1}$
usually k = 0 or k = 1
You can get the sum of N terms from a geometric sequence via algebra
Sum of N terms 
$\frac{a(1-r^n)}{(1-r)}$ if r $\not=1$
$Na$ if r = 1

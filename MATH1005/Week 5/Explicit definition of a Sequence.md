Explicit definition of a [[sequence]]
An explicit definition of a sequence is a formula for $a_{n}$
tell what timestep you're at, will return what value the sequence takes. Does not rely on previous values, or in other words, is not defined recursively. 
$\forall _n \in \mathbb{N} \ a_{n}= 2^n$

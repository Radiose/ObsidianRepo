Counting
Counting can be viewed as finding the number of members of some [[Set]], also known as the size of a [[Set]]

![[cardinality]]

Counting the elements of a set, or determining the [[cardinality|cardinality]] of the set of natural numbers from 150 - 160.
Create a bijection with the rule f(x) = 159-x
thus the [[cardinality|cardinality]] of the set is 11.

A harder example: 
How many numbers from 150 to 330 inclusive are [[congruence modulo]] to 5 mod 7?

150 mod 7 = 3
5 mod 7 = 5
thus 152 mod 7 = 5 
159 mod 7  = 5 ... 

Rule: Subtract 5, divide by 7, subtract 20

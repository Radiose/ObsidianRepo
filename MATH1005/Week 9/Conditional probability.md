 Conditional probability 
This is the form of probability most closely associated with the key word *given*. For example, what is the probability event A occurs, given that event B also occurred? 
The probability of A given B in a [[Sample space]] S is denoted as the following:
$A,B \in S$
$\mathbb{P}(A|B)=\frac{\mathbb{P}(A \cap B)}{\mathbb{P}(A)}$


### Continuous case
$f_{Y|\mathbf{X}}(Y|\mathbf{x})=\frac{f_{\mathbf{X,Y}}(\mathbf{x},Y)}{f_{\mathbf{X}}(\mathbf{x})}$ - same idea, divide the joint by the marginal.

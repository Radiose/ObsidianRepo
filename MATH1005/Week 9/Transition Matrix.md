Transition [[matrix]]
A [[Transition diagram]] can also be shown in a more useful form, that being the transition [[matrix]].
Transition matrices can be thought of as the following: what is the [[Probability of an event]], where we move from the state in the column to the state in the row 

$\begin{bmatrix}0.8, 0.6 \\ 0.2, 0.4\end{bmatrix}$ state 1 to state 1 - 0.8, state 2 to state 1, 0.6 etc

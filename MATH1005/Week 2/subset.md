subsets
$a \subset b \iff \forall x(x\in a \implies x\in b)$
You can say that the subset is the analogous equivalent of implies. 
You can prove this by showing that a fixed but arbitrary element of A, must also be in B
You can disprove this by demonstrating that there exists an element of A that is not in B.

Note that its not a subset if A = {1,2,3} B = {{1,2,3},{1,2}}
A is a **member** of b, not a subset

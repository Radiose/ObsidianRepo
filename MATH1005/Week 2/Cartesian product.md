Cartesian product
Recall the definition of a [[Set]]
In many situations, the order in which data appears is important, and the same data is sometimes represented multiple times. 

for example, in the coordinate plane, order matters.
![[Ordered n-tuples]]

The cartesian product of n-set
Given (not necessarily distinct) sets A1, A2, . . . , An, the Cartesian
product of A1, A2, . . . , An, denoted A1 × A2 × · · · × An, is the set of all
ordered n-tuples (a1, a2, . . . , an) where a1 ∈ A1, a2 ∈ A2, . . . , an ∈ An.

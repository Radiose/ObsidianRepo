A digit in base 2 is called a bit
a block of 8 bits is called a byte
a block of 4 bits is called a nibble
a sequence of several adjacent **bytes** is called a word. The number of bytes varies, depending on the purpose of the word. For example, a 2 byte word can store non negative integers in the range from 0 to $2^{16}-1$.

Representing positive and negative integers in a computer
One idea is to represent numbers using a fixed number of bits, and then add a 1 or 0 at the first bit to indicate negative or positive 
EG $(00000001)$ = 1
   $(10000001)$ = -1
   This is a bad idea because it uses a positive or negative 0. This is not good.

A better idea is to follow a scheme called twos complement

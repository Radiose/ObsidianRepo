congruence modulo - can be done by defining an [[equivalence class]] on the [[set]] of integers 

$aR_{d}b \iff (\exists_{k} \in \mathbb{Z} \ a=b + kd)$

This is creating a [[partition]] over the set of integers.

**Two numbers are congruent modulo IFF their difference is a multiple of d**
**This also also written as**
$a \equiv b \ (mod \ d)$
to mean $aR_{d}b(mod \ d)$

two ints are congruent modulo IFF they leave the same remainder upon division by D

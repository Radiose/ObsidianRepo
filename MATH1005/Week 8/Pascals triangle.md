Pascals triangle
This is a simple method to understand the Binomial theorem. Frequencies in coin tossing correlates to pascals triangle. Each row is generated by expanding a binomial. The first row is what occurs when you expand out an expression $(x+y)^0$, the second is $(x+y)^1$....
![[Pasted image 20260503162152.png]]

The value in pascals triangle correlate to the coefficients of the polynomial, while the exponents will always add up to the original exponent applied to the binomial. 

$(x+y)^4 = y^4+4y^3x+6y^2x^2+4yx^3+x^4$

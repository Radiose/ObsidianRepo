Discrete  
When every event has the same probability, the resulting densities and distributions are called **uniform**

Continuous 

The uniform distribution for a continuous random variable similarly can be determined by 
$\mathbb{P}(X\leq x)=\frac{x-a}{x-b}$ for $x \in[a,b]$

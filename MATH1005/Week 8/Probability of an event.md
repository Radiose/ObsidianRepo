Probability of an event
The probability of an event is denoted via $\mathbb{P}(E)$  



Let S be a finite sample space in which **all outcomes are equally likely**
Let E be an event in S 


**Then the probability of the event E is $\mathbb{P}(E)=\frac{|E|}{|S|}$, where these refer to the [[cardinality]] of E and S.**

For any **finite** sample space: 
$\mathbb{P}(E) \in \mathbb{R}$ between 0 and 1 
Probability of the complement is $1 - \mathbb{P}(E)$
 $\mathbb{P}(E) =1$ means certain 
 $\mathbb{P}(E)$ = 0 means impossible

composition of [[Function]]s
$(f \circ g) = f(g(x))$
Important note is that the order of them is of ultra importance.

Note that function [[composition of Functions|composition]] does not associate properties. 
If $(f \circ g)$ and f is g is [[Injective Function|injective]] and f is [[surjective function]], f g will only be surjective sometimes. 
This means that you cannot say $(f \circ g)$ is surjective, because the definition of surjective uses for all.

directed graphs
a directed graph is a set A of vertices together with a subset $r \subset A\times A$ of directed edges. if $(x,y)\in R$ we say that there is a directed edge from x to y. When a is small, a digraph can be drawn with the vertices as points and directed edges and arrows.



OR

A directed graph is defined as a [[set]] of vertices, along with a [[multiset]] of [[Ordered n-tuples]] with size 2 (ordered pairs) of endpoints. 
Loops are still allowed
The edges of a [[directed graph|digraph]] are sometimes called *arcs*
The direction of an arc is given by an arrow

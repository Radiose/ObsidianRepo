Walk 
a walk is a [[Sequence]] of vertices of a [[graph]] alternating with edges. $v_{0}e_{1}v_{1}e_{2}v_{2}\dots e_{n}v_{n}$
A walk starting at $v_{0}$ and ending at $v_{n}$ is said to connect $v_{0}$ to $v_{n}$
The **length** of the walk is the number of edges listed. 
A **trivial walk** contains no edges, thus has a length of 0.

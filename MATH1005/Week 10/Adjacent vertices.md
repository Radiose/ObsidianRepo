Adjacent vertices 
U,V are adjacent if $\{ u,v \} \in E(g)$. They are basically adjacent if they have an edge between them.

These adjacent vertices can be represented as an adjacent matrix, where $a_{i,j}$ represents the amount of vertices between I and J on the [[graph]].

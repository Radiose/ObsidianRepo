Bipartite graph
A bipartite [[graph]] is a [[Simple graphs and digraphs|simple graph]] where every edge of the connects from a [[set]] A to a set B, and A and B are [[partition]]s of $V(G)$ . 



## Complete Bipartite graph
$\forall a \in A\ \  \forall b \in B$, the edge $\{ a,b \}$ belongs to the [[graph]]
There are no other edges in the graph 
The complete bipartite graph is denoted as $K_{m,n}$, where m is the number of vertices of A, and n is the number of vertices from B
The complete number of edges is m x n

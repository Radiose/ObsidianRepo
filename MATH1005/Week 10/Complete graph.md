Complete [[graph]]
A complete graph on n vertices is a [[Simple graphs and digraphs|simple graph]] in which each pair of distinct vertices are adjacent. A complete graph on *n* vertices is denoted by $K_{n}$

The amount of edges can be calculated by $\begin{pmatrix}n  \\ 2\end{pmatrix}$

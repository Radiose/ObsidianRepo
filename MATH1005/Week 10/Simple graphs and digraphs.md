Simple graphs and digraphs
A simple graph is a [[graph]] that has no loops, and no parallel edges.

A simple digraph is a [[directed graph|digraph]] that has no loops and no parallel edges 
Note that having $(a,b)$ and $(b,a)$ is ok, but having $(a,b)(a,b)$ is not.

Disjunctive normal form
suppose $p_{1}, p_{2}\dots$ are statements

a compound statement is in disjunctive normal form when it is a disjunction ($\lor$) of conjunctions $\land$. 

For any statement form that is not a contradiction, in the truth table for the statement form, we may replace the statement form by a statement in disjunctive normal form so that the truth table remains correct.

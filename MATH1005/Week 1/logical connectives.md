**logical connectives**
and/not/if/if and only if/ if then

**Complexity** builds by combining statements with logical connectives

some compound statements:
if 2 divides 4238, and 3 divides 4238, then 6 divides 4238


Often, natural language does not allow for a compound statement to be completely clear. This is why mathematical language does not sound perfect.


Australia is in the southern hemisphere **and** tropical storms spin clockwise in the southern hemisphere.

Statements can be introduced under a letter, similar to variables in algebra.

*p*: Australia is in the southern hemisphere

Variables can represent arbitrary statements. Whena  variable represents an arbitrary statement, it is called a statement variable.
A statement form is when statement variables are cojoined with logical connectives


Various logical connectives have their own symbols. These symbols must be used in the correct order and placement mathematically.

The truth value of a statement form depends on the truth values taken by the statement variables.

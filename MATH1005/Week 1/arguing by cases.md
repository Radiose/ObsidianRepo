arguing by cases
if the domain of a [[predicate]] is partitioned into subsets, you may prove a $\forall$ statement by proving it for each subset

for example:
prove the following [[Statement]]: 
for all integers x,$x^2+x+6$ is even
For this statement, you may prove it by first proving that the predicate holds true for all even numbers. 

You may then prove it by showing that the predicate holds true for all odd numbers

How to prove things

Before trying to prove a [[Statement]], you should clearly identify the logical structure of the statement. This allows you to understand the choices you have.


Proof of $\forall x p(x)$
let x be a (fixed but arbitrary) element of the predicate domain. Argue that p(x) is true.

Tips for proofs:
Before starting a proof, clearly identify the logical structure of the statement being proven. 
Consider your options for a logical structure that will prove the [[Statement]]
write down the logical structure of your argument so that the reader understands whats going on
when deciding between a direct argument and argument via the contrapositive, try whichever direction appears to allow you to make the strongest supposition first. The same advice applies when considering a [[proof by contradiction]] or one of the other methods.

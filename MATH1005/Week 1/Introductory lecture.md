Discrete mathematical models: Abstract representations of processes and objects, the steps or units of which can be indeed by a subset of the positive integers. In particular, we avoid continua like the open interval (0,1).

For example, all real numbers between 0 and 1 are not included, as they are continuous and cannot be accurately indexed. 

Computing is run on discrete mathematics. Abstract model of the memory of a computer: 0 or 1
	Discrete time - clock cycles. 


Sudoku for example, is a great example of a process that can be modelled with discrete mathematics.

Google has utilised discrete mathematical models via linear algebra to create a page ranking algorithm.

$\sqrt{ 2 }$ is negative or non negative.

Tropical storms spin clockwise in the southern hemisphere. The statement is an instance of the statement form $p \implies q$, where p is in the southern hemisphere, and tropical storms spin clockwise.

Some statement forms are true, no matter what values that statement variables have:

FOR EG: 6 is even or 6 is not even. 

$p \lor \neg p$ is always true, therefore it is a **tautology**


The opposite of a tautology is called a contradiction. These will be be false no matter what. EG: australia is in the southern hemisphere and not in the southern hemisphere. There is no possible statement variable that will output a true value.

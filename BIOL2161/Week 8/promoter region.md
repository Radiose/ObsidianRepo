promoter region 

[[Eukaryotic cells]]:

A region in the DNA that defines where gene transcription starts, where general transcription factors, GTFs assemble, and where [[RNA polymerase II]] binds.

Promoter activity is the most important factor that determines regulation of gene expression.  

Promoter activity is regulated by: 
Chromatin accessibility 
Transcription factor binding 
Promoter sequence itself. EG tata box.

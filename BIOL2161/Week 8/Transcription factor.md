Transcription factors
These modify chromatin at specific genes via response to cellular signals. They directly bind to DNA. Any other regulatory proteins act indirectly via modification of chromatin, or interacting with [[Transcription]] factors. 

All transcription factors are proteins. they regulate gene expression by binding to specific DNA sequences, acting as switches to turn genes on or off. 

TF can be repressor, or activator. 
transcription factor binding sites are specific, short sequences of DNA recognised and bound by transcription factor proteins to regulate gene expression .


activator protein 
A very important protein in [[Transcription]] that provides gene specific regulation. Each [[Gene]] uses a different activator protein

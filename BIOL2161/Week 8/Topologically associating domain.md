Topologically associating domain 
aliases: TAD 
[[Chromatin]] is organised into regulatory regions, each about 100kb-1mb long. Chromatin state of each TAD can be regulated independently. 
These were discovered in 2012. 
Tads are 3d genomic domains that contain multiple chromatin loops. These loops bring distant genomic elements into close proximity. 
![[Pasted image 20260430162559.png]]

TADs contain [[Gene]]s and their regulatory elements. 
TADs are typically defined by boundary elements. These are the "insulators"
Most [[Transcription]] regulation occurs within a TAD, but sometimes it occurs between TADs. These exceptions are typically less frequent and are usually more constrained.

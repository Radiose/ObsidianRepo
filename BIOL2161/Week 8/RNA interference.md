RNA interference 
This is a post [[Translation in Eukaryotic cells]] [[Gene regulation]]mechanism of [[mRNA]] in cytoplasm. It is a process in which [[RNA]] molecules guide protein complexes to specific [[mRNA]], leading to degradation or inhibition of [[Translation]].

It is sequence specific, and primarily acts in the cytoplasm to regulate mRNA. In some organisms, it can also effect [[Transcription]] though.

miRNA and siRNA

They key part is that they are basically the same structure. They difference between whether a gene is attenuated or completely switch off comes completely down to whether a double stranded RNA has complete or partial complementation. 

# miRNA
microRNA - used for fine tuning of gene expression. It primarily acts via [[Attenuation]] of [[Transcription]]. Generally present in [[Eukaryotic cells]]. Generally originate from the genome. 

It is primarily transcribed by [[RNA polymerase II|pol II]] from a *genome locus* (miRNA encoding [[Gene]]). They are then processed by an RNAse enzyme named **drosha** into pre-miRNA 

the pre-miRNA forms a hairpin structure due to partial sequence complementation (regions that are not perfectly complementary to each other in the DNA dont bond together properly).

![[Pasted image 20260502170234.png]]

It then enters the cytoplasm, and is processed by a dicer which "dices".








# siRNA
small interfering RNA - these are stronger than miRNA. They either switch a gene completely on, or off. Present in plants, fungus, animals, not so much in mammals. 

These arise from double stranded RNA - which arises due to post [[Transcription]]al pairing or processing. There are no dedicated siRNA genes, in comparison to miRNA which does have well defined origins. The double stranded RNA can have endogenous or exogenous origins (viruses)
It is then processed by a dicer which dices the DSRNA to produce a short double stranded siRNA. It will then be loaded into the **RISC complex**

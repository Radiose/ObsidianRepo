RISC complex (RNA induced silencing complex)

A complex of proteins that together with [[miRNA and siRNA]], will interact with [[mRNA]] in the cytoplasm.

miRNA or siRNA duplex is loaded into the RNA induced silencing complex. One strand is is retained, while the other is removed. The strand that is kept will aid the complex into locating the particular sequence of [[RNA]] that will be silenced. 

**Argonaut** is the protein that will actually silence gene from being expressed in the [[mRNA]].

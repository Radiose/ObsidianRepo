Expression plasmids 
Requires:
- Multiple cloning site - flanked by promoter and terminator Sequence - often contain restriction sites as well - needs operator as well 
-  Origin of replication on the [[plasmid]]
- Has a selectable marker

Very often, the [[T7 RNA polymerase]] promoter is used  

Standard [[Lac operon]] promoter, TAC, TRC, T5 as recognised as well but not used as often 

LAC operator sequence is often used in the expression plasmid

Genes from any protein from any organism can be put into an [[Expression plasmid]]

Entire [[Operon]]s can be put it 

Molecular cloning methods to create these plasmids include 
Restriction enzymes and ligation,
Gibson assembly, 
Golden gate

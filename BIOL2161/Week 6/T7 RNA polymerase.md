T7 RNA polymerase 
The DE3 prophage is the most popular expression system
DE3 prophage: phage DNA inserted into [[Bacterial Chromosome]] 
*DE3 prophage* expresses T7 RNA polymerase under control of lacUV5 promoter and operator, which can be regulated by the lacI repressor in the [[Lac operon]]

The lacUV5 promoter differs at two nucleotides. The change makes the lacUV5 promoter much stronger for t7 RNA polymerase 

Once bacteria creates T7 RNA pol through  [[Transcription]], this set up [[Expression plasmid]]

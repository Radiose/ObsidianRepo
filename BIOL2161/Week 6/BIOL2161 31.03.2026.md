Recombinant protein 
-Usually done because we want to do something with it and need lots of it. 
- Examples include solving structures, functionally understand it, isolate it, utilise it for something else.
- Sometimes we want to study proteins from organisms that are difficult to work with. We cant simply purify it, so we must recombinantly express it to study it further. 
- Design a "new to nature" protein and study its function

Factors to consider for recombinant expression 
Codon usage
Usually, we have to optimise codon when going from [[Eukaryotic cells]] to [[prokaryote cells]] and vice versa, as some codons have more tRNA genes and are used more than others that code for the same protein 

Fate of protein 
Adding/removing signalling sequences help control where protein ends up due to transport etc 

Processing of [[mRNA]] transcript - eukaryote genes have introns that should be removed 

Cysteines content 
Cysteine residues can cause disulphide bonds, creating incorrect structure. Solution is to express with DSB proteins 

Post translational modification 
Often influence proteins structure and function, can be solved by mutating sites or using different expression system 

Disadvantages to using [[Escherichia coli|E. coli]] 
Sometimes a protein expressed is toxic to E.coli 
	Use different strain or system with greater repression/weaker expression 
Doesnt work so well for eukaryotic proteins

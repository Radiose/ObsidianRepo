DNA polyermase I
replaces [[RNA]] primers during [[DNA replication]]

Has exonuclease activity in 5-3' direction 
Removes one RNA nucleotide from 5' end as one DNA nucleotide is added to 3' end of the preceding [[Okazaki fragments]].

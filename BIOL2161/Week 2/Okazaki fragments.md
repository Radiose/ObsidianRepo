Okazaki fragments
short [[DNA]]  fragments, synthesized on lagging strand, with each fragment requiring a separate DNA primer to be created.
formed because DNA polymerase synthesizes DNA only 5'-3'
each fragment requires a separate RNA primer 
RNA primers are later removed are replaced with DNA 
DNA fragments are joined together by Ligase to seal the backbone(phosphodiester bonds).

Length in [[prokaryote cells]] - around 1000-2000 nucleotides 
Length in [[Eukaryotic cells]] - around 100-200 nucleotides

[[RNA]] primers have to be used. This is because DNA polymerase cannot start DNA synthesis *de novo*. it needs primase, which makes RNA primers.

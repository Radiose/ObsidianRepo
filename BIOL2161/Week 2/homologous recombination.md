homologous recombination
This mediates exchange of genetic information between homologous chromosomes
It uses an intact homologous DNA molecular as a template for accurate repair - looks for other strand 
Evolved as a double strand break repair mechanism 
In meiosis, programmed [[Double stranded DNA break]] initiate recombination - thought to evolve to enhance genetic diversity

Recombination provides a means to generate new combinations of genes 

During [[homologous recombination]], big blocks of genes are exchanged at the [[chiasma]]. 

It is a mechanism for generating genetic diversity but also functions in DNA repair. 

During a [[Double stranded DNA break]] on one of the chromosomes, the break can be repaired using a sequence on the other chromosome. This creates ![[holliday junctions]]

The removal of these holiday junctions is what creates genetic diversity. 


Crossovers accompany repair by resolving holliday junctions 
A crossover is formed when there is one vertical cut on the [[DNA]], and one horizontal, this happens mostly in [[Meiosis]].

![[Pasted image 20260305105753.png]]

A noncrossover appears when both strands are cut symmetrically. 

Noncrossover repair is normally mediated by *blooms helicase* during [[Mitosis]]. This is why it is more common in [[Meiosis]].


Single [[holliday junctions]] can help bypass replication fork blockages by fork regression. 

Note that this is caused by **nonhomologous end joining**

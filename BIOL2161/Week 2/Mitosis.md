Mitosis
A diploid organism has two sets of chromosomes organised into homologous pairs. 
At times, a chromosome consists of a single chromatid, other times it consists of two sister chromatids, joined by a constricted region of the chromosome known as the centromere. 
Mitosis produces genetically identical daughter cells, each with the same amount of genetic material. 
prophase : condensation of chromosomes 
metaphase: aligned in the metaphase plate 
anaphase: chromatids seperate

Mismatch repair
During replication process, mismatched bases are incorporated into the new [[DNA]] with a frequency of 10^-4 to 10^-5

Most of the errors are corrected - by proofreading processes 

Incorrectly inserted nucleotides or corrected by mismatch repair 

steps: Incorrectly inserted nucleotides are recognised
Mismatch repair enzyme cut out section of the newly synthesised strand and fill the gap using the original strand as a template

MSH, MLH proteins in humans
MUTS MUTH MUTL proteins in bacteria

newly synthesised DNA strand is discriminated from the old strand by the lack of methyl groups

Deamination
spontaneously occurring process
causes [[Base mismatches]] via the loss of amino group from a base.

Example: cytosine losing an amino group turns it into Uracil. The amount of hydrogen bonds is also changed. If Guanine and Cytosine are together, they have 3 hydrogen bonds. If cytosine is converted into uracil, it bonds with Adenine, with only 2 hydrogen bonds.

Deamination occurs spontaneously, but the rate can be increased by some chemicals.


Another example. 5-Methylcytosine becomes deaminated to thymine, changing the number of hydrogen bonds between bases. 

Deamination of bases leads to point [[mutation]]. During [[DNA replication]], each strand becomes separated. From here, A new strand of mutant [[DNA]] will be created, while another will become normal.

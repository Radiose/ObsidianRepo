Implications of [[Deamination]] of methyl cytosine. 
63% of mutations found in human internal organs result from [[Deamination]] of methylated cytosine. These represent cases where the initial [[Deamination]] event was not detected and repaired so the mutant strand is replicated, meaning the change is inherited by one daughter cell.
This indicates that repair by [[Base excision repair]] is good, but not perfect

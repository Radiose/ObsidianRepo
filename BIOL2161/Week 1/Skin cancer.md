Skin cancer is a great example of DNA mutations caused by environmental factors. UV light causes [[mutation]] in the DNA. Accumulation of DNA mutations cause cancer.
![[Pyrimidine dimers]]

80,000 pyrimidine dimers per cell induced in the human epidermis after 1 hour of sunlight. Although most are repaired, the repair machinery can be overwhelmerd

If [[mutation]] occur in the germline cells, they are heritable and are passed to the next generation. They can occur at random, or be created through radiation or chemical exposure. 

For each single nucleotide in the genome, theres a 1 in 100,00,000 chance it will change when passed to the next generation.

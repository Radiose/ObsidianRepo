The phosphate group in DNA is a 3 phosphate group. 

DNA has a double helix of **complementary antiparallel chains**. 

Adenine and thymine have 2 hydrogen bonds, while GC have 3 hydrogen bonds each

It is significantly easier to unwind DNA through the nitrogenous base hydrogen bonds rather than the covalent phosphodiester bonds

DNA has a right handed helical structure, because this conformation optimizes base stacking interactions and minimises STERIC HINDERANCE

The helical shape protects information from damage by reducing exposure to outside molecules. It also is not uniform. There are alternating major and minor grooves due to the angle at which bases attach to the sugar phosphate backbone.

Nucleotide excision repair

Cuts away the damaged strand
DNA polymerase uses intact strand as template
Ligase seals backbone

Detects and repairs bulky lesions in DNA 
complex process in humans, involves over 30 proteins 

UvRA can recognise the damage - ultraviolet repair ATPase 

This UvRA recruits UvRB  when it detects a problem

UvrB is a Single strand
binding protein that binds
and helps to keep DNA
strands separate from each
other

UvRC - endonuclease making dual incisions, 12-13 long damaged oligonucleotide

UvRD - removes damages section with helicase activity 

Missing section is repaired with DNA Ligase

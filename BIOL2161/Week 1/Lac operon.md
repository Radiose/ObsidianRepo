Lac operon
Has 3 structural genes, LacZ, lacY, lacA. 
Singular promoter region 
Lactose itself is comprised of two sugars *galactose* and *lactose*

Lactose needs to be transported into cell and broken into its sugars
Done via *permease*

The product of lac Z gene is *Beta-galactosidase.* 
Can convert lactose into allolactose
Can break down lactose and allolactose into galactose and glucose

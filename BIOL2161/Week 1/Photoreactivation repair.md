Photoreactivation repair
Organisms that have photoreactivation repair use photolyase enzyme
This is not in humans
It is sensitive to visible light (350-500nm)
Photolyase undergoes conformational change, and absorbs light and breaks the bond in the [[Pyrimidine dimers]].

Photoreactivating enzyme uses energy from visble light to break the bonds between two pyrimidine residue

direct repair involves reversing the chemical reaction causing the DNA damage

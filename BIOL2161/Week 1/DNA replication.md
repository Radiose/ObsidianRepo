DNA replication
It is catalysed by DNA polymerase, which is a very accurate enzyme
New nucleotides are added onto 3'OH groups. So DNA sysnthesis occurs in 5'-3' Direction
Nucleoside triphosphates are the substrates for DNA synthesis. Pyrophosphate is released and the nucleoside monophosphate is released and the nucleoside monophosphate is joined onto the growing strand.
A nucleoside is an unattached nucleotide
dNTP are always added to 3' end, where the triphosphate is cut to release energy and assemble the backbone

Sense and antisense strands
The sense strand will have the same sequence as mRNA, but with T instead of U
The antisense strand is on the bottom and is also known as the template strand. This is what mRNA is copied from. It is also known as the template strand.

During [[DNA replication]], the antisense trmplate strand is copied continously as the leading strand, while the sense strand is copied via short okazaki fragments as the leading strands
Therefore, the distinction between the Sense and the antisense strand is determined by the direction in which RNA/DNA polymerase moves along the DNA during transcription

An okazaki fragment requires an RNA primer

In [[prokaryotic cells]], okazaki fragments are significantly longer, and can be up to 2000 base pairs long. COME Back

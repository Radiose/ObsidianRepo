The complete set of DNA in an organism, including [[Gene]]s and non coding sequences.

 ![[Genome structure]]

In [[prokaryotic cells]], genome size is pretty strongly correlated with number of genes. Generally around a thousand genes per million base pairs. 
![[Genome evolution]]

In eukaryotes, 20-25,000 genes, with the addition of [[Co transcriptional processing]] and [[Post translation regulation]], result in over a million proteins possible. 

The complexity of an organism is dictated by the amount of combinations of proteins and exons, not more genes.

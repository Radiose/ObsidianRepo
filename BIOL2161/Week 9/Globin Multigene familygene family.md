Globin [[Multigene family|gene family]]
Haemoglobin in the blood carries oxygen and consists of four globin proteins. Two copies of each $\alpha$ and $\beta$ and the cofactor haem. 
Foetus specific haemoglobin required - higher affinity for oxygen, is expressed through $\gamma$ globin gene. 

Evolution over time: Primordial globin gene underwent a duplication event.

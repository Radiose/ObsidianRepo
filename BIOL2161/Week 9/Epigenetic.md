An example: Bees 
If royal jelly is present, a larva will become a queen bee, otherwise will be a worke rbee 
This is because the DNA methyl transferase 3 will be repressed, thus the queen genes will be able to be produced, as its [[Gene]]s will be active. 

Another example:
Dutch Hunger Winter 
The kids that in utero had their mothers starving. Thus, the babies developed a genotype that lead to a slower metabolism. Then, when they became adults, they developed obesity, impaired insulin secretion, transcriptional regulation. These changes were purely [[Epigenetic]].




### inactivated x chromosome 
In placental mammals: whether you have one or two x [[Chromosome]]s can lead to [[Epigenetic]] regulation of an x chromosome. *Xist* will shut down one of the chromosomes in organisms that have two x chromosomes, leading to appropriate levels of [[Gene]] expression. There is no favouring of the maternal or the paternal X [[Chromosome]]. The Tsix gene is complementary to the Xist gene, so the Tsix gene inactivates the Xist gene. Come back. 


The lyon hypothesis:
Black and orange colours in fur are covered in different X [[Chromosome]]s. The cats will have spots of black and orange randomly in the body. It shows that different cell lineages in the body will have different inactivated X chromosomes.

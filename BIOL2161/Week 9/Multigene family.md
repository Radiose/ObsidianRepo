Multigene family
A multigene family is a set of genes that arose by duplication of ancestral [[Gene]] and subsequent divergence due to small changes in the nucleotide sequence.

A multigene family may contain pseudogenes that are homologous but dont produce a functional protein because of mutations.

# [[Genome evolution]] of gene families 
If a [[Multigene family|gene family]] is useful, we will see lots and lots of copies of it in a plant. Typically gene duplications are lost, as there is no selection for them.

Plant fungus example:
Some fungus act as pathogens on plants. (can also be bacteria). They infect a plant via secreting lots and lots of protein into the plant environment. Some of them go outside, some of them go inside the cell. These proteins bind to plant proteins, changing processes within the cell. These cause cell death. 
One event thats occured is the exchange of exons in the plant through recombination. Resistance receptors (plant immune machines) get exons from targeted protein added onto them, so that now the receptor has a protein domain which already interacts with the effector, allowing it to find the pathogen faster.

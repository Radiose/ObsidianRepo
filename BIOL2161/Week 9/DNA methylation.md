DNA methylation

This is methyl groups being directly added to the *cytosines* in the [[DNA]].
First epigenetic mechanism to be discovered. 
At position 5 in the cytosine [[Purine]] structure, DNA methyl transferases will add or remove a $CH_{3}$ methyl group.

Problem: [[DNA replication]] will lead to a fresh cytosine being created without methylated. 
Thus, an enzyme DNA methyl transferase 1 will add the correctly methylated group to the other strand via sensing on one strand.

Adding methyl groups will reduce the amount of [[Transcription]] at these regions.


DNA methylation is always repressive. It will always block recruitment of [[Initiation of transcription in Eukaryotic cells|transcription machinery]]



[[RNA]] directed [[DNA methylation]]

This is when the short [[miRNA and siRNA|siRNA]] end up in the nucleus and bind to [[DNA]]. When that occurs, it recruits certain [[DNA methylation]] enzymes. This has evolved because there are lots of viruses that insert themselves into our DNA and try to express a gene very highly. The methylation is a natural protection to prevent excessive gene expression.

[[Chromatin]] remodelling 
They key idea here is that [[Chromatin]] can only be accessed when in the open form(euchromatin).
This process is **dynamic** - [[Nucleosome]]s can be unwound. **Chromosome remodelling complexes** do this via ATP usage. Transcriptional regulation is all about accessibility.

[[Nucleosome]] octamer: h2a h2b h3 h4 create a hectomer and two of them create an octamer.

Lysine: main amino acid that will be used COME BACK 

Histone tails:
Tails of histones contain alot of important charged residues that bind to DNA. Modification of these charges. 

Acetylation of [[histone]]s. Addition of an acetyl group to lysine will remove positive charge on lysine. Leads to less condensed [[DNA]] which is available to [[Initiation of transcription in Eukaryotic cells|transcription machinery]].

HATS : add acetylation 
HDs: remove acetyl groups 
process is reversible and dynamic. 


Acetylation will always increase [[Initiation of transcription in Eukaryotic cells|transcription machinery]], but other forms of chromatin remodelling can be repressive.

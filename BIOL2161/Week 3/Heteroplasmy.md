Heteroplasmy
Presence of more than one type of [[Mitochondria]]l [[DNA]]
There can be two types of [[Mitochondria]]l DNA- wildtype and mutant in a cell
Heteroplasmy levels can vary between tissues because [[Mitochondria]] are randomly distributed during cell division. 
During inheritance, a mitochondrial genetic bottleneck in the maternal germline can cause large shifts in the heteroplasmy levels between generations - results in members of the same family showing different disease severity.

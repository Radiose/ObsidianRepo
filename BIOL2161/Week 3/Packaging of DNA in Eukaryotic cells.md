Packaging of DNA in [[Eukaryotic cells]]
each chromosome consists of a single continuous DNA molecule. eukaryotic [[DNA]] associates with histones to form **chromatin.**

 

Levels of chromatin structure
1:double stranded helix 
2: [[Nucleosome]] 
3:nucleosomes fold up and produce 30nm fibre 
4:fiber loops of 300 nm in length 
5: the loops are compressed and folded to produce 250nm wide fibre 
6:coiling of the 250 nm fiber produces a chromatid of a [[Chromosome]]

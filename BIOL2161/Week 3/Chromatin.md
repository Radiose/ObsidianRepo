Chromatin
Chromatin is the material that comprises [[Chromosome]]s. It is described as a dense mixture of [[DNA]] and proteins([[histones]]) that package long DNA molecules into compact, dense structures within the nucleus. 

The fibre that builds the [[DNA]] together. 

Euchromatin is the extended form of chromatin.  - loosely packed for [[DNA replication]]
Heterochromatin is the condensed form of chromatin - tightly packed - inactive form

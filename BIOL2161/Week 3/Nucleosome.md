Nucleosomes 
regularly organise [[DNA]], allowing it to fit inside the nucleus. These are DNA packed with histones


Nucleosome organisation


Core histones(form octamer) of nucleosome are comprised of h2a, h2b, h3, h4. ALl of these have 2 copies. 

Around the octamer, ~147 base pairs of DNA wrap 1.65 times  to form the [[Nucleosome]]. 

Histone H1 binds to the nucleosome and has a structural role
![[Pasted image 20260312171313.png]]



Each nucleosome is an octamer of two copies of each of the histones.

![[histone]]



Non histone proteins form a chromosome scaffold.

Cohesin and condensin are two major proteins involved in chromatin looping and condensation, respectively.

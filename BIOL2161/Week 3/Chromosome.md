Chromosome
[[DNA]] is packaged with histone proteins, so that a chromosome contains roughly equal amounts of [[DNA]] and protein 

Small stretches of DNA wrap around histone octomers to form nucleosomes 

![[Nucleosome]]



Chromatin regularly organises [[DNA]], but remains accessible for transcription and [[DNA replication]].

Higher order folding of nucleosomes form 30 nm fibers and loops, allowing further compaction

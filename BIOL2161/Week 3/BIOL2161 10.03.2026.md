[[DNA structure]] and organisation requires stability, [[Protection against mutations]], and accessibility. 

[[Eukaryotic cells]] have much larger genomes and more sophisticated mechanisms for DNA packaging

DNA wraps around histone proteins to form nucleosomes, the basic unit of chromatid packaging. 

Higher order folding allows efficient packaging in the nucleus.

DNA packaging is dynamic - can be loosened or tightened 

[[Escherichia coli|E. coli]] bacterium
E coli DNA is 1000x longer than the cell itself. 4.6 million base pairs. 
4000 protein coding genes.

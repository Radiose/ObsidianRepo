Mitochondrial replacement therapy
 Helps prevent inherited mtDNA disorders
 basic idea - replace defective mtDNA with healthy donor DNA from both parents
Methods
Pronuclear transfer:
egg cell from mother and donor cell fertilised at the same time, remove nucleus from defective cell into donor cell - discard zygote from healthy donor
Maternal spindle transfer:
Transfer nucleus from mother cell into healthy donor cell. Fertilisations happen after mitochondria has been exchanged.

Mitochondrial dynamics
They move around the cell alot - exchange lots of material with each other. 

[[Chloroplast]] genome
Usually around 134,000 base pairs 
~110-130 genes total 
Mitochondrial genome in plants 
186,000 bp - 19 million - much much bigger 
only encodes 40-50 proteins 
lots of junk and repeated sequences. 

Mutations in plant organellar genomes - 
Can cause defects in chlorophyll production 
Mitochondrial can cause cytoplasmic male sterility

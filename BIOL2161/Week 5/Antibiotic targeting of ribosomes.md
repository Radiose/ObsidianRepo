Antibiotic targeting of ribosomes 
Tetracycline
Blocks binding of aa-tRNA to the A-site of ribosome 

**Streptomycin** - prevents transition from initiation to change elongation - causing miscoding event 

**Chloramphenicol** - blocks peptidyl transferase reaction on ribosome 

**cycloheximide** - blocks translocation 
**kanamycin** - binds to 30s subunit - results in incorrect alignment with mRNA

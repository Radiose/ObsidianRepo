Translation 
steps:
charging of tRNA - attaching an amino acid onto tRNA 
aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase 
enzyme that drives process - one for each AA- ATP required

Each amino acid will have its own tRNA synthetase. This molecule binds tRNA, and amino acid and linked them together. This process requires ATP
Creates a very high energy bond, that will become broken by ribosome during translation itself 


the last base on the tRNA is always adenine - will link to carboxyl group on amino acid 


Specificity of aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase

These aminoacyl tRNA synthetase ensures specificity through 
recognition of unique structural elements in tRNA and Amino acids

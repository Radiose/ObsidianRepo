## Initiation:
the presence of the [[5' cap]] in [[Eukaryotic cells|eukaryote]] [[mRNA]] means that initiation is different. 
There is no shine dalgarno sequence as there is in prokaryotes. There is instead, the **kozak sequence**. 
![[Pasted image 20260425175818.png]]

The small [[Ribosomal subunit]], together with initiation factors will bind the [[5' cap]] and attach to the [[mRNA]].


The 40s complex will then scan along mRNA until it encounters AUG starts codon. 
This AUG start codon is recognition is determined by the surrounding sequence, which is the kozak sequence.

A strong kozak sequence will lead to efficient initiation 
A weak one will lead to possible leaky scanning 

When AUG is recognised, Met-tRNAi base pairs with start codon. Initiation factors are released, the large [[Ribosomal subunit]] is recruited and joins. 

After assembly, the full system enter elongation, synthesising the polypeptide. 

### Role of poly-a-tail in translation. 

The [[poly a tail]] is bound to the ribosome itself. It helps to ensure mRNA stability 

Ribosome recycling: an idea about ribsomes desintegrating, then very quickly coming back and reinitiating.

TRP [[Operon]]
Tryptophan - an essential amino acid - used in the biosynthesis of proteins 
a trp operon has 5 different genes

Is repressible (default on) operon under negative [[Transcription control|control]] (uses repressor)

 High presence of tryptophan within the cell will cause repressor to bind, blocking [[Transcription]]. 

![[Attenuation]]


In [[Eukaryotic cells]], each coding sequence produces one gene. Each has one single promoter and terminator etc. Much more extra work. Much more flexible

In [[prokaryote cells]], TRP is an [[Operon]]

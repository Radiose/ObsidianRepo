Attenuation 
tryptophan biosynthesis is also regulated by attenuation
A mechanism for reducing expression of [[TRP Operon]] when tryptophan has high presence by preventing the completion of [[Transcription]]

RNA polymerase will stop prematurely during [[Transcription]],  only a short mRNA is made that does not encode any trypophan biosynthesis enzymes

[[Attenuation]] is dependent on coupling - the translation of [[mRNA]] will start while it is still being transcribed

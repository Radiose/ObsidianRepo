elongation of translation in [[prokaryotic cells]] 

AA are joined to create a polypeptide side chain 
Ribosome attached [[initiation of translation in prokaryote cells]] - now additional tRNA can come 

Each ribosome has 3 sites 
- A site: 
	- Aminoacyl
- P site:
	- peptidyl 
- E site:
	- exit site
initiator [[tRNA]] (formyl methionine) occupies P site 
all other [[tRNA]] enter A site 


Three initiation growth factors 
EF-TU (elongation factor thermo unstable)
- a prokaryotic elongation factor thats responsible for catalysing the binding of an aminoacyl-tRNA to the ribosome 
- G protein (binds GTP) and facilitates the selection and binding of an amino acid tRNA to the A site of the ribosome 
EF-T
regenerates active EF-Tu, after GTP hydrolysis to GDP
EF-G
stimulates translocation of ribosome to the next codon 


Step 1
N methylformanthionine in P site 
EF-Tu, GTP, charged [[tRNA]] form complex. Complex enters A site. Anticodon/codon pairing 
GTP cleaved, results in release of EF-TU-GDP which gets recycled by EF-T

Step 2
Peptidyl transferase forms peptide bond between amino acids in A and P site.
[[tRNA]] in P site release amino acid 
Step 3:
**EF-G** and GTP move ribosome to the next codon (called translocation)
Empty tRNA is now in E-site and is detached 
A site can now receive next tRNA

A bacterial pathogen is binding to a plant cell with a type 3 secretion system - like a straw 
Straw traffics proteins in - these effector proteins have a transcription factor like binding ability - can bind to eukaryotic genes to change  - these are called TALEs (transcription activator like effectors

A transcription activator like effector contains a DNA binding sequence that can theoretically target any DNA. These novel DNA binding proteins are naturally occuring [[Transcription factor]]s from the plant pathogen xanthamos 

Tal effectors from Xoo are injected into a type III secretion system into plant cells where they bind effector binding elements in cognate SWEET(carbohydrate related genes) host gene promoters, resulting in increased expression of SWEET genes that make sugar more available to the pathogen 

Natural or chemically induced(both random), or site specific gene editing mutations in effector binding elements can lead to disease resistance

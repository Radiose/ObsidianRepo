Genetic modification 
Direct manipulation of organism DNA using modern biotechnology methods to alter its characteristics such as adding, deleting or editing genes 

In plants, GM refers to the insertion of foreign DNA into the plant genome, with the resulting product called a GMO 

Examples: 
Bt cotton: Cry1AB and Cry1Ac produces a protein that acts as an insecticide. 

## Creation of a GM plant 
We need plant promoter, kozak sequence, and terminator to stop [[Transcription]]
We start from a [[plasmid]], and clone the set of genes 
We then use agrobacterium tumefacians - this is a tumourous bacterium that inserts its own DNA into the plant genome 

Modifications of TI plasmid as vector : 
We remove opine metabolism 
We maintain minimal virulence region 
we change T-DNA into gene of interest 


For monocot plant species that arent compatible with agrobacterium, we coat DNA and RNA with gold and deliver it into cells directly using a gene gun. Both plasmid and linear DNA can be used, and once in the nucleus, DNA can be randomly incorporated into the chromosome 

Generation of TG tissue

We use tissue culture, and insert antibiotic genes to screen for it after transformation. We then screen via PCR to complete the forward genetic screen

Transposable elements
These are [[DNA]] elements that can move around the [[Genome]]. 
Scientists saw the heritability of genes were not stable. Genes that were close together typically were inherited together. Scientists thought that elements were getting moved around in the gene. 

 DNA transposons are moderately repeated, mobile DNA sequences that are spread throughout the [[Genome]]s of [[prokaryotic cells]], plants and animals alike.   You can observe between individuals that different numbers were present and that they occupied different spaces in the genome 

Transposons range in size from hundreds to a few thousand BP 
The sequences are cut and inserted into a new site in the genome by the process of **transposition**

44% of the human genome is comprised of [[Transposable elements]], corn has 85%, fruit flies have 3%. However, much of the transposable elements we and fruit flies have are inactive. 

There are two classes of [[Transposable elements]]
# Class 1(retrotransposons)
Retrotransposons operate via a *copy and paste* mechanism
This is done simply by creating an RNA transcript which is then reverse transcribed into [[DNA]] - done via [[RNA]] intermediate

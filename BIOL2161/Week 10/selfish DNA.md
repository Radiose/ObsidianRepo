There are alot of these in [[DNA]]. These are mobile elements that dont produce critical functions themselves but replicate to high numbers in the [[Genome]] over evolutionary time. 

Many of these are incomplete and non functional but some continue to transpose. In some cases, the movement of these can cause disease. There are over 60 cases of disease due to insertion of retrotransposons. 

## Importance
Its been increasingly recognised that [[Alu element]]s and other similar elements are crucial to evolution. They can alter gene expression and function by introducing new sequences into existing transcription units

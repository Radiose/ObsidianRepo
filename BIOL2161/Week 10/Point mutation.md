Point [[mutation]]
In exons, they are an important driving force in [[Genome evolution]]. [[Exon shuffling]] is much faster, but point mutations are still important. 
Nonsense mutation: A stop codon being created via a change in codon structure.   
Missense mutation - new codon codes for a different amino acids 
Silent - no real change 
Frameshift - insertion or deletion of a number of nucleotides that is not divisible by 3. The most catastrophic .  


point mutations in non coding DNA can alter [[Gene regulation]], primarily through things like binding of [[Transcription factor]]s or RNA Pol and splicing 
In non functional DNA there is no effect.

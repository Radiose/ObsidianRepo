
Done at the end of [[Transcription]]

this is what occurs during [[Termination of transcription in Eukaryotic cells]] that creates a poly a tail and allows cleavage of RNA into pre [[mRNA]] and [[RNA]] that will be degraded later.

Process 
The transcript contains a specific [[Consensus sequence]] AAUAA located near the future 3' end. 

It is an [[Introns and exons|exon]], but is located in the untranslated region. 

The AAUAA sequence is the signal that tells [[RNA polymerase II]] where to process the transcript. 
The pre mRNA is cleaved 13-30 nucleotides from the [[Consensus sequence]], and the cleavage defines the new end of the [[RNA]] molecule. 

After cleavage, a long stretch of Adenines to the new 3' end, creating the ![[poly a tail]].

Protein domain
A protein domain is a region of a protein with a specific structure or function. It often folds independently of the rest of the protein.

A key thing to understand is that the amount of protein domains present in an organism, its about the way they are **arranged**.

Proteins will share domains across each other. During [[Genome evolution]], having [[homologous recombination]] that shifts a protein domain to join to another [[Gene]] is a way more effective evolutionary step than trying to mutate [[DNA]] to generate a new functional protein.

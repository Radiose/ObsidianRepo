RNA polymerase 
# In [[Eukaryotic cells]]
This is an enzyme that is highly involved in the process of [[Transcription]] in both [[prokaryote cells]] and [[Eukaryotic cells|eukaryote]]s . 
In prokaryotes, there is one form of RNA polymerase. 
In [[Eukaryotic cells|eukaryote]]s ,there are 5 
- pol I - pol III in all eukaryotes.
- pol IV + V in plants

#### RNA polymerase I
Transcribes [[rRNA]] genes, produces ribosomal RNA 

![[RNA polymerase II]]

#### RNA polymerase III
transcribes small structural RNA. Produces transfer RNA, [[tRNA]], and other small noncoding RNA, such as 5s [[Ribosomal subunit]] [[rRNA]]

### Plant only RNA polymerases
#### RNA polymerase IV
Produces RNA that become small interfering [[RNA]] (siRNA)

#### RNA polymerase V
produces scaffold [[RNA]] used for hetero[[Chromatin]] formation

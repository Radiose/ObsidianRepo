Gene 
Genes have multiple definitions 
Mendelian definition of a gene 
A gene is a unit of heredity that determines a trait and is transmitted from parent to offspring

Molecular definition of a gene 
A gene is a sequence of [[DNA]] that is [[Transcription]] into [[RNA]] and in many cases, translated into a functional product (usually a protein)

Functional definition of a gene 
A gene is a region of [[DNA]] that produces one ore more functional [[RNA]] molecules, together with regulatory sequences



Genes are also known as [[Transcription unit]]s

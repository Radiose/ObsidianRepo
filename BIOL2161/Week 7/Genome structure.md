Genome structure 
[[prokaryotic cells]] usually have circular [[Bacterial Chromosome]], and typically has polycistronic [[mRNA]] as a result of [[Operon]]s

In [[Eukaryotic cells]], nucleus is membrane bound. Has multiple linear [[Chromosome]]s. Genes are mostly separate, with lots of introns and exons. Single [[Gene]]s in eukaryotes have their own [[promoter region]]s. Genes are also very far apart.

For example, the [[TRP Operon]] in bacteria has all genes together closely. In [[Eukaryotic cells|eukaryote]]s, they are spread over 4 different [[Chromosome]]s.
Eukaryotic genes can be very similarly regulated to eukaryotes, but they dont need to be together. It also allows much more control over regulation, IE more of TRP1 than TRP 2, but no TRP 3.

Spliceosome

Built from 5 snRNP's (small nuclear Ribonucleoproteins)
In [[Alternative splicing]], 
Each snRNP has a unique role 
U1: Recognises 5' splice site
U2: positions the branch point adenosine 
U6: forms catalytic core with U2
U5: aligns the exons for ligation 
U4: has regulatory role over U6
There is no U3

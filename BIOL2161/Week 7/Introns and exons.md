Intron 
Regions of DNA which do not code for anything. Present in [[Eukaryotic cells]]
Exon: coding region 

Introns are highly variable in size, 3-5kbp.
Exons are typically 100-200 bp in size. 

[[Gene]]s are usually 24-30 kpb but are highly variable

For this reason, most of genome structure in [[Eukaryotic cells]] is introns. 

Transcripts include 5' and 3' and untranslated regions

Enhancer proteins are a sequence that increases [[Transcription]] via binding to [[Transcription factor]]s


Enhancer is a region where activator TFs bind to TF binding sites. 

Silencer is a region where repressor TFs bind 
both enhancer and silencer regions. 

TF binding sites role as an enhancer or silencer is dictated by the cluster of TFs it binds. 
This role is not fixed, but is context dependent, and can change during cell lifespan, cell type and development of organism.

Enhancers and silencers can be either *proximal*, or *distal*. Their effect on transcription does not exactly depend on their physical location, but instead, their strength of effect. 
Typically, closer enhancers are more effective, but the cause and effect is not direct. Instead, more effective enhancer and silencer sequences are typically closer.

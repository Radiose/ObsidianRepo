
This is done very early on, right after the first RNA molecule has been added.

Newly synthesised [[RNA]] has a 5' triphosphate end on its first nucleotide. 

5' cap is added via: 
- one phosphate removed
- guasonine nucleotide is added, making the end of it GpppN (where n is the first nucleotide)
- Methylation of newly added guanine base 
- methylation of ribose sugars on the first (sometimes second and third as well) nucleotide. 
- Sometimes, the base of the first nucleotide may be methylated itself.

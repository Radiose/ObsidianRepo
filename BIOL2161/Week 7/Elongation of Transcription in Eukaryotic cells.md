Elongation of Transcription in [[Eukaryotic cells|eukaryote]] cells
After 30 base pairs, [[RNA polymerase]] has left the promoter region and is now elongating [[mRNA]] using DNA as the template 
![[Pasted image 20260423165335.png]]

1: DNA enters the polymerase through the cleft. 

2: Inside the cleft, [[DNA]] strands separate, with one strand becoming the template strand 

3: RNA synthesis at the active site. RNA nucleotides (rNTPs) enter through the funnel. 

4: Newly synthesised RNA is then bound to the DNA strand, before becoming separated via a wall of amino acids. 

 5: once exiting RNA polymerase enzyme, DNA reanneals.

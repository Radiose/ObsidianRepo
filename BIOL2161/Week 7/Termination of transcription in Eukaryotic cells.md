Termination of transcription in [[Eukaryotic cells]]

[[RNA polymerase II]] transcribes past the [[Gene]]

RNA is cleaved at the 3' end, which is decided by the poly-a-tail. The upstream RNA gets the poly a tail, and will later become mature [[mRNA]]. 

[[RNA polymerase II]] keeps transcribing though, creating a trailing RNA product.
Rat 1 exonuclease degrades the trailing RNA by binding to 5' end of RNA and degrading it towards Pol II.

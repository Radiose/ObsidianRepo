[[Transcription]] of [[Eukaryotic cells]]
Quite similar to [[prokaryote cells]] cells, with the addition of introns and exons. Introns must be removed via extra processing of [[mRNA]] to undergo [[RNA splicing]].


Initiation of transcription in [[Eukaryotic cells]]
A [[Consensus sequence]] TATA box is binded to by a TBP (tata binding protein) and transcription factor II D.

TFIIA, TFIIB stabilise TBP-DNA binding

TFIIE, TFIIF, TFIIH bring in [[RNA polymerase II]] and complete pre-initiation complex

TFIIH  then [[unwinding DNA]] around the start site to create transcription bubble 

Mediator protein (large complex of proteins that is specific to each [[Gene]]) acts as a bridge between gene specific regulatory proteins(repressors etc), and the core [[Transcription]] machinery (pol II).

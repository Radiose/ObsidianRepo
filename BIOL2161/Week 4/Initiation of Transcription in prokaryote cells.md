Initiation of [[Transcription]] in [[prokaryotic cells]]
An RNA polymerase holoenzyme will 
- bind to consensus sequences
- unwind dna - [[Transcription]] bubble
- Adds nucleoside triphosphate complementary to DNA to synthesis mRNA
- New nucleotides will be added to 3'  end of [[RNA]]
- during bond formation - **pyrophosphate** will be released 
- sigma factor released as polymerase moves beyond the promoter region

Directionality
Promoter orientation determines template directionality
RNA is always transcribed from 5'-3' from the template strand from 3'-5'. So travels along the template strand3'-5' and adds 5-3
Therefore, RNA sequence will be identical to the non template strand, just with U and T replaced

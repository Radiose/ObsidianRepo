Rho dependent termination
- **[[Rho]]** protein disassociates RNA polymerase and moves it off the template
	requires:
	- DNA region to slow polymerase down - not super specific 
	- Unstructured region to provide binding site for [[Rho]] protein - rho utilisation site 
	Mechanism:
	- polymerase will reach site and slow down [[Transcription]]
	- [[Rho]] will then activate and catch up - unwinds DNA RNA hybrid with [[Helicase]] activity 
	- **doesnt require hairpin**

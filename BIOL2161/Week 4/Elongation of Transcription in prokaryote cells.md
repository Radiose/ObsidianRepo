Elongation of [[Transcription]] in [[prokaryote cells]]
Dna is unwound as polymerase moves along strand. Nucleotides are added to 3' end of mRNA. As this occurs, the DNA is rewound upstream.

Bacterial transcription occurs at about 14 nucleotides a second compared to [[DNA replication]] in bacteria which is 1000-2000 nucleotides a second (at 37 degrees celcius).

RNA/DNA features influence speed. Includes secondary structures of the RNA molecule and specific sequences 
The [[Transcription]]al pausing is important.

This process is usually highly accurate - RNA polymerase can delete last 2 nucleotides it added if necessary
Transcription bubble is normally about 18 nucleotides long. At any point, 8 RNA nucleotides are bound to DNA template. 

[[Topoisomerase]] is used to release stress and tension in unwound DNA

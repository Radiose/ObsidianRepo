Intrinsic termination (rho independent termination) 
- [[RNA]] forms a **hairpin structure** which displaces RNA polymerase 
- terminator has 
	- inverted repeats 
	- string of adenines
	- ![[Pasted image 20260321201805.png]]
		- inverted repeats will be transcribed into [[RNA]]
	-  Adenines will slow down polymerase, allowing for formation of hairpin structures
	- DNA-RNA binding is destabilised and [[Transcription]] is terminated

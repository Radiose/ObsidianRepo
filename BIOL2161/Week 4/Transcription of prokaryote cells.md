[[Transcription]] of [[prokaryote cells]]

Three stages of [[Transcription]] 
Initiation of transcription in bacteria 
two stages 
Binding - occurs at promoter region 
Initiation - Involves [[unwinding DNA]] to expose a single strand in the region of binding and clearance of the promoter - creating a transcription bubble.
Promoter regions in [[Escherichia coli|E. coli]]
Essential sites for transcription to occur at
Has - 35 consensus sequence, and -10 consensus sequence along 5-3' strand 
Are 10 and 35 base pairs away from transcription start site
Not every promoter will have the same consensus sequence

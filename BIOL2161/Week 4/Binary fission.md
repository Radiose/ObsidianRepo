Binary fission
A kind of asexual reproduction utilised by [[prokaryote cells]]. 
The general process:
DNA replicates, and each circles origin of [[DNA replication|replication]] will be drawn to the poles of the bacteria. Cell elongates, and then basic division occurs. A very fast process. [[Escherichia coli|E. coli]] can divide once every 20 minutes.

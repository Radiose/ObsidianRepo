Central dogma of molecular biology

DNA -> RNA  -> Protein 

The most essential part is that there is no nucleus, so translation of an mRNA may occur before transcription is complete.

[[DNA replication]] takes around 40-60 minutes, but [[Escherichia coli|E. coli]] can replicate every 20 minutes. Because of this, [[DNA replication]] must begin before the previous round of replication has finished. 

This occurs via something called multifork replication. This only occurs during fast growth stage, as opposed to slow growth which [[DNA replication|replication]] concludes before [[Binary fission]].

Transcription
The conversion of [[DNA]] to [[RNA]]. 
Process is about getting information out of DNA into a more useful form for the cell
DNA is a code for the production of RNA. 
RNA is a messenger - mRNA
linker - tRNA
translator - rRNA
many other roles - regulator - scaffolder - detector - enzyme

RNA contains a second hydroxyl group on the 2' carbon atom compared to DNA.

RNA contains thymine instead of uracil 

thymine - has an extra methyl group that uracil doesnt
This makes uracil less stable than thymine 

DNA is double stranded vs RNA is usually single stranded 

RNA can fold up on itself via complementary base pairing. This leads is to be capable of forming secondary structures.

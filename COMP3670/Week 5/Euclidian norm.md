the [[Euclidian norm]] measures the length of a [[vector]].
$||\mathbf{x}||_{2}=\sqrt{ \sum_{j=1}^d x_{j}^2}$


The Euclidian distance between two vectors is $d(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})=||\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}||_{2}$, where a smaller distance can indicate similarity 

Note, there are other norms that can be used. 
For $p \geq {1}$, we define the $\ell_{p}$ norm as $||\mathbf{x}||_{p}=\left( \sum_{j=1}^d |x_{j}|^p \right)^{1/p}$, with our [[Euclidian norm]] defined as the case $p=2$
Each norm induces a distance as well, defined in a similar way to the [[Euclidian norm]]

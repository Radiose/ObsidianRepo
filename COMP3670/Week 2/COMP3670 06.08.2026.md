Topic:
Probability and statistical learning theory 
 The idea: how to use probability theory to determine how much we have learnt 

Use a probability distribution to model new, unseen data 
Use a [[conditional distribution]] which says how the outcome depends on probabilistic way of the features 
A probabilistic bound - likelihood $E_{out}(\hat{h})-E_{in}(\hat{h})$ is small 

Within the [[supervised learning]] framework, the outcome is not completely determined by the input - same test results may or may not have the disease

Features simply do not contain all information about the outcome 
The outcome is best described as random - data generating process can be thought of as a random phenomenon. We use probability theory to aid us. 

In this course, we define [[Sample space]] as $\Omega$ and each outcome is denoted as $\omega \in\Omega$. Recall an event is a subset of the sample space.

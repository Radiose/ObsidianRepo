In machine learning, we have to deal with several inputs at once, so we make a random vector

A random vector is a [[function]] $\mathbf{X}:\Omega \to \mathbb{R}^n$, $\mathbf{X}=(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})$ with each coordinate itself being a [[random variable]]

The joint distribution is described by a joint distribution which can be described by a probability mass function, or a continuous [[Density function]].

Discrete $p_{x}(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})=\mathbb{P}(X_{1}=x_{1},\dots,X_{n}=x_{n})$. Literally the definition of [[Joint probability]].

# Mathematical notation
$\mathcal{D}=\{ (\mathbf{x_{1}},y_{2}),\dots, (\mathbf{x_{n}},y_{n})\}$ with $\mathbf{x_{i}}\in \mathcal{X}$ and $y_{i}\in \mathcal{Y}$
Hypothesis set: $\mathcal{H}$ (set of candidate functions $h:\mathcal{X}\to \mathcal{Y}$)
In sample error: $E_{in}(h)$ - the error of $h$ on $\mathcal{D}$
$E_{in}(h)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{1}(h(\mathbf{x_{i}})\not=y_{i})$
in which $\mathbb{1}\{ \cdot \}=1$ if the expression inside is true, and $\mathbb{1}\{ \cdot \}=0$ if false. 
Learning from data:

$\hat{h}=\text{arg min}_{h \in \mathcal{H}}E_{in}(h)$-find the rule that minimises the error.

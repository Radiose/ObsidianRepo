This is the form of learning that involves observation of only inputs. $\mathbf{x_{1}},\mathbf{x_{2}},\dots,\mathbf{x}_{n}$ with no labels at all. The task is typically to discover some structure hidden in the data. 

Some common tasks:
- Clustering - groups similar points
	- Customer segmentation - discover groups of customers with similar behaviour, with no predefined categories. 
		$\mathbf{x}=$ purchase history, browsing behaviour etc
- Dimensionality reduction - compress data while preserving structure. 
	- genomics: compress into a small number of variables that capture the sources of variation
		 $\mathbf{x}=$expression levels of thousand of genes per patient 
- Density estimation: Model the distribution of $\mathbf{x}$
	- fraud detection 
		- $\mathbf{x}=$ transaction amount, location, time, merchant.

Hoeffdings inequality 
If you have independent, and idenditcally distributed random variables, taking values in $[0,1]$, with $\mathbb{E}(W_{i})=\mu$, then for every $\epsilon>0$, $\mathbb{P}\left( |\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N W_{i}-\mu|>\epsilon \right)\leq 2e^{-2N\epsilon^2  }$
This goes to $0$ very fast. In other words, the sample average of bounded, indepdnent, identically distributed quantities is close to its expetation, and the probability of being far decays exponentially

Making a mutable generator 
When you have immutable data, its easier to understand what code is doing.
Once you have a variable assigned to a value, you can just substitute that value in every subsequent part of the code, as it will never change.

When you have mutable data(such as [[Array]]), you have to start reasoning about what pointers are pointing to what at what instances in time.

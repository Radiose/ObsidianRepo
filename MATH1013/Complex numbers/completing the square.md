completing the square
Note that we use this for solving quadratics with [[Complex number|complex]] roots in this case 
$x^2 -2x +4 =0$
$\implies x^2-2x = -4$
$\implies x^2 -2z + \left( \frac{2}{2} \right)^2= -4v + \left( \frac{2}{2} \right)^2$ - note that we are adding $\left( \frac{b}{2} \right)^2$ 
$\implies x^2-2x +1=-3$
$\implies (x-1)^2 =-3$
$\implies (x-1)= \pm\sqrt{ -3 }$
$\implies x - 1 = \pm\sqrt{ 3 }i$
$x = 1 \pm \sqrt{ 3 }i$

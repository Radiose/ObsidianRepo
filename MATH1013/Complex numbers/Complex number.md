A complex number is one from the [[set]] $\mathbb{C}$
The uses for complex numbers include: 
- Solving arbitrary polynomial equations where the solution is not real
- quantum physics, in particular the Schrodinger equations

Recall that [[The determinant]] of a polynomial indicates the quantity of real roots that are present to a quadratic equation 
We add on an additional [[Statement]], that if $b^2 < 4ac$, then the quadratic will have 2 distinct complex roots. 

We define the set of complex number as follows 
$\mathbb{C} = \{ x+yi |x,y \in \mathbb{R}\}$

Each complex number $z$ will have two components: 
The real component, defined $\mathrm{Re}z$ and the imaginary component  $\mathrm{Im}z$

We can now define the following operations 

## Addition 
$z_{1}=x_{1}+y_{1}i$
$z_{2} = x_{2}+x_{1}y$
$z_{1}+z_{1}=(x_{1}+x_{2})+(y_{1}+y_{1})i$

## Multiplication 
$z_{1}=x_{1}+y_{1}i$
$z_{2} = x_{2}+x_{1}y$
$z_{1}z_{2} = (x_{1}x_{2}-y_1y_{2})+(y_{1}x_{1}+x_{1}y_{1})i$

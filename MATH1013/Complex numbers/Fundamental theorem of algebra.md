The fundamental [[theorem]] of algebra is as follows 

Any degree $n$ polynomial with [[Complex number|complex]] coefficients can be factored into the product of $n$ degree 1 polynomials with [[Complex number|complex]] coefficients. 

In other words, and $p(x) =a_{n}x^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\dots+a_{0}$,     with  $a_{n},a_{n-1}\dots a_{0} \in \mathbb{C}$

$p(x) = a_{n}(x-x_{1})(x-x_{2})(x-x_{2})\dots(x-x_{n})$, where $x_{1},x_{2}\dots x_{n} \in \mathbb{C}$

In other words, if $a_{n} \not=0$ and $a_{n},\dots,a_{0} \in \mathbb{C}$
then $a_{n}x^n + a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\dots+a_{0}=0$ has n roots (counting multiplicity)
valued in [[Complex number]]s

(Note that all real numbers are a subset of the complex numbers )

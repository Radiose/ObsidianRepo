For any [[Complex number]] $z = x+yi$, 
$z$ can be viewed as a vector in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $x = \mathrm{Re}z$ and $y = \mathrm{Im}z$
z can be specified uniquely by its magnitude r and its angle with the positive x axis.

[[The magnitude of a vector]] can be calculated for complex numbers via $\sqrt{ x^2+y^2 }$, this is also known as the modulus of z, denoted $|z|$

$\theta$ is called the argument of Z, denoted $arg(z)$

A complex number can be defined as $z = r \ cis(\theta)$, where $cis\theta = \cos \theta+ i\sin \theta$

## Geometric representation of multiplication 
We brute force multiply
$z_{1}z_{2} = r_{1}r_{2}\ cis\theta_{1}cis\theta_{2} = r_{1}r_{2}cis(\theta_{1}+\theta_{2})$

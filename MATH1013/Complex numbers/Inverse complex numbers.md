Some useful notes about inversing complex numbers 
If z is outside of a unit circle, $z^{-1}$ is in 
if $z$ is in, $z^{-1}$ is outside 
if z is on the unit circle, then $z^{-1}$ is as well 

## Algebraic inversion 
This is based upon a trick, where we note that $z \bar{z} = (x+yi)(x-yi)=x^2-(yi)^2=x^2+y^2$ 
Thus, $z^{-1} = \frac{1}{x+yi}=\frac{x-yi}{(x+yi)(x-yi)} = \frac{x-yi}{x^2+y^2}=\frac{x}{x^2+y^2}-i \frac{y}{x^2+y^2}$

Separable ODE
# Definition 
An ODE of the form $y' = f(x)g(y)$ is separable 
To solve it, we turn it into two differentiable problems 
$y'=f(x)g(y)$
$\frac{dy}{dx}=f(x)g(y)$
$\frac{dy}{g(y)}=f(x)dx$
$\implies \int \frac{1}{g(y)}dy=\int f(x)dx$
This creates an *[[implicit definition of a sequence|implicitely defined]]* ODE, as we have y = f(x) which can be obtained via the [[Inverse function]] and [[Natural logarithm]]s.

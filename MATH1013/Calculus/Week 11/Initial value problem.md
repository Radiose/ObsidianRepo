## Initial value problems 
An IVP is a problem comprising an [[ordinary differential equation|ODE]] and some initial conditions, which are conditions of the form $y(x_{0})$ or $y'(x_{1})=z_{1}$. The general solution to this ODE involves some arbitrary constants. We use the initial conditions to determine the values that the constants must take.

### Example
$y'=x^3$ subject to $y(2) =6$
The ODE is asking us to antidifferentiate. 
$y = \frac{x^4}{4}+c$
$6 = \frac{2^4}{4}+c$
$y = \frac{x^4}{4}+2$

	Indefinite integral 
The [[Indefinite integral]] is the [[Indefinite integral|antiderivative]] of a [[Function]]. 

The [[Indefinite integral|antiderivative]] of a function is a function such that the derivative of that function is equal to the original [[Function]].


Properties of the [[Indefinite integral]]
$\forall k \in \mathbb{R}$
$$\int k(f(x))=k\int f(x)$$
$$\int f(x)+g(x)dx = \int f(x)dx + \int g(x)$$
$$\int f(x)- g(x)dx = \int f(x)dx + \int g(x)dx$$

The closed interval method 
to find the global [[Maxima and minima]] of a [[continuous function]] on a closed interval \[a,b]
steps 
Find all [[Critical point of a function|critical points]]  of f in (a,b) (find points where f'(x) = 0 and f'(x) DNE)
Evaluate f at a and b and all the [[Critical point of a function|critical points]]
The largest value found is the global maxima, while the smallest is the global minima taken, both taken from the closed interval \[a,b]

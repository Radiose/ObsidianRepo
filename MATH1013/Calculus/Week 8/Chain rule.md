Chain rule
chain rule: applies to composed functions ![[Pasted image 20260310104344.png]]

If there are more than two [[Function]]s composed in each other, for example 

$f(x)=\sin(\cos(\ln x))$
$(f \circ g \circ h)' (x)$ = $f'(g \circ h) (x) \times (g \circ h)'(x) = f'(g(h(x))) \times g'(h(x))\times h'(x)$

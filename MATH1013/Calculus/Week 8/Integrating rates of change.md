Integrating rates of change

## Units 
When adding or subtracting properties that have associated units, they typically must be of the same type of unit. IE adding 3 minutes + 10 meters is nonsensical.

However, multiplying properties with associated units leads to units being multiplied together.

This same logic holds for [[Riemann sum]]s. 
$\sum_{i=1}^nf(x_{i}^*)\Delta x_{i}$
units of Riemann sum = units of $f(x) \times$ units of $x$.

## Net change 
due to [[The fundamental theorem of calculus|FTC]] II, $\int_{a}^b F'(x)dx =F(b)-F(a)$
If $F'(x)$ is described as the rate of change of F with respect to $x$, then **the integral of a rate of change over an interval is the net change over that interval**.

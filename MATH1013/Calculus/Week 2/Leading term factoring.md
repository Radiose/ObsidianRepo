The other method would be to use polynomial division.


Not all functions approaching infinity have the same magnitude. 

$e^x$ will approach faster to infinity than any polynomial.

The leading term of a function is the function that approaches infinity the fastest. Even if the limit is not to infinity. This makes sense when you consider 1/x will approach infinity, while x^10 will approach zero.

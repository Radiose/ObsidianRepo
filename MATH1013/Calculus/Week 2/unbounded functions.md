unbounded functions 
It may happen that as x approaches a, f(x) gets arbitrarily large and positive, or arbitrarily large and negative.

Eg $f(x)=\frac{1}{x^2}$ when x approaches 0 = $\infty$

$\lim_{ x \to 0 } \frac{1}{x}$ is infinity when approaching from above, and -infinity when approaching from below 

if the limit approaches positive or negative infinity from above or below, we say the function has a vertical asymptote

mathematical model
A simple, abstract and idealised description in mathematical language of an object, system or relationship.

Because an object is abstract and idealised, and described in mathematical language, we can reason about it mathematically. 

Our model must be simplified to give us a chance of saying anything, but there is an obvious trade off. Too simple and it will not represent the aspect of the system well, but not simple enough and we cannot make progress in the study of the model. *Our simplifications are often in the form of assumptions that we know are not true, but eliminate complexity*

Making a mathematical model that is simple enough and explanatory requires insight and skill. It is a skill that makes you powerful and valuable. In this course, you will see many example of models and you will make some models yourself.

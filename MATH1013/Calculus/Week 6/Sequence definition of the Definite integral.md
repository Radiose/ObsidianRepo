Sequence definition of the [[Definite integral]]
Given a [[Sequence]] $x_{1},x_{2}\dots x_{n}$ of real numbers and a real number L, we write $\lim_{ n \to \infty }x_{n} = L$ if for every $\epsilon \in (0,\infty)$ there exists a positive integer M such that $n>M \implies |x_{n}-L|<\epsilon$

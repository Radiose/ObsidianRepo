Differentiable approximation
Suppose that y=f(x) from some differentiable [[Function]] f and fix a point a in the domain of f
A small change a = a+dx from *a* will produce a corresponding change $dy = f(x+dx)-f(a)$. We call dx the **differential** of x. It is an independent variable. We call dy the **differential** of y. 


That is, since 
$f(x)-f(a) \approx f'(a)(x-a), we\ have\ \ \ dy \approx f'(a)dx$

whenever dx is small.

Related rates
this is an important application of [[derivative]]s. If Q(t) is a [[function]] that varies with time, then the derivative $\frac{dQ}{dt}$ gives the rate of change of that quantity with respect to time. 
When quantities are *related*,  a change in one necessitates a change in one another, and the rates of change are related.

Related rates often require simply tuning an equation to act as a function of something else. EG making an equation a function of x is when you make it proportional to x. 

Its important to note that related rates are not the same as typical [[Implicit differentiation]], as differentiating things in relation to time requires implicit differentiation on every variable, not just x.

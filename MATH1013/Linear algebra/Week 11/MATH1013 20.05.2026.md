# The crown jewel of first sem linear algebra 
Show that for a square [[Matrix]] A, the columns of A are [[linearly independent]] $\iff$ the rows of A are [[linearly independent]] 

Since A is a square matrix, the columns of A are [[linearly independent]] $\iff \det A \not=0 \iff \det A^T \not=0 \iff$ the rows of A are [[linearly independent]].

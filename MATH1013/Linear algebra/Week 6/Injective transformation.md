For the linear transformation [[Function]], is it [[Injective Function|injective]]?
is $T(x_{1}) \not=T_(x_{2})$?
$Ax_{1} \not= Ax_{2}$
$A(x_{1}-x_{2})\not=0$? - very important due to [[linearity]]

Is $x_{1}-x_{2}$ in the [[Null space]] of A?

iF Null(a) = $\vec{0}$, then for $x_{1},x_{2} \in \mathbb{R}^n$, 
If ($Ax_{1}=Ax_{2}$), then $A(x_{1}-x_{2})=\vec{0} \iff x_{1}-x_{2}\in Null(A)$
Therefore, if the [[Null space]] of A is 0, then T is [[Injective Function|injective]]

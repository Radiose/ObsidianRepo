Spanning $\mathbb{R}$
two [[vector]] will [[span]] across all $\mathbb{R}$ as long as they are not proportional to each other. EG $\vec{v}_{1}\begin{pmatrix} 2  \\ 3\end{pmatrix}$ and $\vec{v}_{2} \begin{pmatrix} 4  \\  6\end{pmatrix}$ do not span the real numbers. They are proportional to each other.

you can plug this into an augmented matrix to double check $\begin{matrix}[2,3  | a]  \\  [1, 1|b]\end{matrix}$.

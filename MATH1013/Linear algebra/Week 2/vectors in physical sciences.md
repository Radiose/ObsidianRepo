vectors in physical sciences 
In physical sciences such as physics, a vector can be percieved as to have magnitude, and direction. The magnitude of $\begin{pmatrix} a   \\  b\end{pmatrix}$ is $\sqrt{ a^2+b^2}$. The direction of $\begin{pmatrix} a  \\ b\end{pmatrix}$ is specified relative to the direction of the x axis. The direction is measured counterclockwise as shown below
![[Pasted image 20260304162304.png]]

Three dimensional vectors can be understood as East, North, Up. As in the 2d cases, the origin is irrelevant.

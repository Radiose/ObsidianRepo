The basis of a [[Linear subspace]]
You can describe any coordinate system simply by stating its basic vector. 
EG $1 \iff [1]$. Once the span of this [[vector]] is the real numbers, then we can write any vector in $\mathbb{R}$ as a linear combination of $\vec{v}$.

Let $H \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ be a [[Linear subspace]] of $\mathbb{R}^n$, then a subset $\{ v_{1}\dots v_{k} \}$is called a basis of H if and only if:
- the set $v_{1}\dots v_{k}$ is linearly independent, 
- span$\{ v_{1},\dots,v_{k} \}$ =H

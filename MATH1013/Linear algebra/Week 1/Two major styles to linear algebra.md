Two major styles to linear algebra:
Algebra:
	write down equations
	seek pattern or structure
	use findings to reduce equations to something simpler 
After completing enough examples, try to summarise your method into an algorithm that you can feed to a computer

2: Geometric
	write down the equations(unless you are a genius)
	Try to plot these equations
	What are the geometric properties?
		Eg: am I getting a straight line, circle sphere etc
	Are the two curves tangent to each other
	How many intersection points do I get?
In the semester, we will go back and forth between algebra and geometric

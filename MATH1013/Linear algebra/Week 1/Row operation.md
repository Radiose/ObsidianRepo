Row operations:
row operations are when you aim to simplify linear systems by conducting simple methods
There are three row operations
E1 = add a multiple of one equation to another equation 
E2 = interchange two equations 
E3 = multiply an equation by a non zero constant 

The key idea is to use elementary row operations to simplify a linear system into one we can solve

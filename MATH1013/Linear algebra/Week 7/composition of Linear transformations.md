[[composition of Functions|composition]] of [[Linear transformation of R n]]s
If $T: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ and $S: \mathbb{R}^m \to \mathbb{R}^k$ are both linear transformations, we can compose them. Then, $S \circ T: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^k$ is a [[Linear transformation of R n]].

$T(\vec{x})=A \vec{x}$ 
$S(\vec{x}) = B \vec{y}$

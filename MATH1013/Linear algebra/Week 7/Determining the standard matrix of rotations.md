Determining the [[Standard Matrix]] of rotations
This is a concept I have struggled with particularly. 

Lets start with rotation by theta radians in $\mathbb{R}^2$
The [[Transformation]] is $T: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ 
$e_{1}=\begin{pmatrix}1 \\ 0\end{pmatrix}$

The result of this is $\sin\left( \frac{\pi}{2} +\theta\right),\left( \cos\left( \frac{\pi}{2}+\theta \right) \right)$
using trig identities, = $-\sin(\theta), \cos(\theta)$

Thus the [[Standard Matrix]] is $\begin{matrix}[\cos \theta, \sin \theta] \\ [-\sin \theta,\cos \theta]\end{matrix}$

Rank nullity [[theorem]]
For any matrix A, the dimension of the [[Column space]] and the dimension of the [[Null space]] is equal to the total number of columns of A.


The column space and the null space of A are subsets of different dimensions
So, they exist in different [[vector]] spaces


Thinking geometrically:

If a matrix A is MxN 
col(A) = [[span]] of column $\subset \mathbb{R}^m$
Null(A) = $\{ \vec{x} \ | \ A \vec{x} =0 \}$ $\in \mathbb{R}^n$

**So the null space of A is therefore the third column of Ax, as it is not used for a two dimensional vector b.**

Subsequence

A subsequence of a [[Sequence]] $a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n}$ is a sequence $a_{i_{1}},a_{i_{2}}\dots a_{i_{n}}$ where $i_{1}<i_{2}<\dots<i_{n}$.

Subsequences of a sequence are analogous to subsets. The subsequences of a sequence include the original sequence itself. Note however, that unlike a [[subset]], the empty sequence is not defined, due to definition of a sequence being a function $f :I \to S$ where $I \subset \mathbb{Z}_{\geq0}$.

If a sequence does not [[Convergence of a sequence|converge]], then the [[subsequence]] may or may not converge. 
For example, the sequence $1,2,3,4\dots$ does not converge, neither does any [[subsequence]].

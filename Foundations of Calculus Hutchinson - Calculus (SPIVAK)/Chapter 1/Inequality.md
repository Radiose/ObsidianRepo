## Edge cases  
To solve inequalities of the form $\frac{2}{x-1} > 5$, note that you cannot just multiply by (x-1) on both sides. This is because the sign of the equation is unknown, and thus we do not know whether the inequality will reverse or not. 

Solving inequalities of the form $3x-1<5x+3<2x+15$ involves solving $3x-1 < 5x+3$ and $5x+3 < 2x+15$ separately.

Solving quadratic inequalities 
$x^2-5x+6<0$
Factoring into $(x-2)(x-3) < 0$
Logically, this product is less than zero IFF only one of the factors is negative
since $x-3<x-2$, we need $2<x<3$.

# Definition 

The set $B(x,R)=\{ y \in \mathbb{R}^n:  \lVert y-x \rVert <R\}$ is denoted the open ball of centre $x$ and radius $R$, and also $\lVert y-x \rVert$ denotes the [[Euclidian norm]].


## Remark 
Consider $n=1$, then, $B(x,R)=(x-r,x+r)$
$n=2$, then $y\in B(x,R) \iff ((y-x) \cdot e_{1})^2+ ((y-x)\cdot e_{2})^2 <R^2$

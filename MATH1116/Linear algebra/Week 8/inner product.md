# Analysis simplification
## Definition 
The inner product of two [[vector]]s $(x_{1},\dots.,x_{n})$ and $(y_{1},\dots,y_{n})$ is denoted 
$$\langle (x_{1},\dots,x_{n}),(y_{1},\dots,y_{n}) \rangle:=\sum_{j=1}^n x_{j}y_{j}$$

Let $\alpha,\beta$ be two choices of basis for a [[vector space]] $V$, of dimension $n$. We define the change of base [[matrix]] $X_{\beta \leftarrow \alpha}$ as the square $n \times m$ matrix whose $i$-th column is contains the coordinates of the $i$-th vector $\alpha_{i}$ of $\alpha$ in terms of $\beta$.
So, $$\text{col}_{i}(X_{\beta \leftarrow\alpha}):=[\alpha_{i}]_{\beta}$$
Basically, some basis vector $\alpha$ can be written as a [[Linear combination of vectors|linear combination]] of basis vectors $\beta$, and from there, we make the column the coefficients.

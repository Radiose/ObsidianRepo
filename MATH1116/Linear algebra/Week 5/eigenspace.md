# definition 
we define the eigenspace corresponding to $\lambda \in \mathbb{F}$ as follows 
$E(\lambda;T):=\{ \text{eigenvectors of }T \text{ with eigenvalue }\lambda \} \cup \{ \mathbf{0} \}$ 
$E(\lambda,T)=\ker(T-\lambda \cdot id)$



Example
$D:\mathbb{F}[x]\to \mathbb{F}[x]$ defined as $D(x^n):=nx^{n-1}$, with $D(1)=0$
Every $f(x)\in \mathbb{F}[x]$ is an [[eigenvector]] with [[eigenvalue]] $\mathbf{0}$
$D^{deg(f(x)+1)}(f(x))=0$, so $E(0;D)=F[x]$

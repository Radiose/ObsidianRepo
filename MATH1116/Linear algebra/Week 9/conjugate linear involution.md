A conjugate linear involution $\iota$ is a [[linear map]] from $V \to V$ such that:
1: $\iota(\lambda \mathbf{v})=\bar{\lambda}(\iota(\mathbf{v}))$
2: $\iota^2=id_{V}$

Given a conjugate linear involution $\iota:V\to V$, we define $\phi:V\to V^V$ $\mathbf{v} \mapsto \langle \_, \iota(\mathbf{v})\rangle$
Note that $\phi(\mathbf{v})$ is a linear functional, as well as a $\phi$ being a linear map itself
